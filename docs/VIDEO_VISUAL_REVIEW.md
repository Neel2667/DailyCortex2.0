**Audit Scope:** End-to-End Full-Length Production Evaluation across Three Distinct Vertical Shorts
**Platform Contract:** YouTube Shorts 9:16 (1080x1920) @ 30 CFR, H.264 / AAC 48 kHz

## 1. Executive Visual Audit Overview

This document provides human-readable visual and cinematic evaluation of all three complete production Shorts. Each video is evaluated on:
1. **Opening Hook (0.0s – 3.0s):** Visual thumb-stopping power, curiosity induction, text clarity, and absence of generic fluff.
2. **Body Choreography & Pacing:** Scene transition fluidity, visual relevance to spoken concepts, motion dynamics, diagram clarity, and telemetry formatting.
3. **Closing Payoff (Final 3.0s):** Cognitive resolution, visual hold time, absence of premature black screen, brand identity integration.
4. **Mobile Safe Zone Adherence:** Zero collision with platform UI boundaries (top channel header, bottom title/sound ticker, right interaction column).
5. **Caption Readability & Dynamic Synchronization:** Word-level karaoke highlighting accuracy, font legibility (>25pt), line lengths, and phrase breaks.
6. **Cross-Video Visual Diversity:** Structural distinction across narrative templates, motion graphics, color palettes, and visual metaphors.
